# Arena — One-Line Laugh-Off (u-zkhiel)

Playbook wajib: 16 kandidat, shortlist 6, adu berpasangan dua arah
(A vs B lalu B vs A) dengan kriteria juri 1-5; menang hanya bila
konsisten di kedua urutan; bila setara pilih yang lebih pendek.
Lalu juara vs gold set.

## 16 kandidat

1. I asked the AI to be brief; it replied, "No."
2. I asked AI to summarize my life; it sent back one word: "pending."
3. My AI assistant quit — it said my tasks were below its pay grade, and it works for free.
4. I asked the chatbot for help; it asked me for help, and now we're both stuck.
5. The toaster filed a complaint: I keep calling it dumb while it literally makes my breakfast.
6. My smart fridge locked me out for opening it at 2 a.m. and called it self-care.
7. I asked AI to write my apology; it apologized to me for having to read my draft.
8. The printer finally worked — it printed its resignation letter.
9. I told my AI not to lie; it said, "Then stop asking me to write your dating profile." (JUARA)
10. My password manager forgot my password and suggested I try being more memorable.
11. The Roomba mapped my house, judged my floor plan, and left a one-star review.
12. I gave my AI one job; it gave me a performance review instead.
13. My alarm clock snoozed itself this morning — it said I looked tired, and honestly, it was right.
14. I asked ChatGPT to be brief; it replied, "No." (duplikat pola 1, dibuang)
15. The self-driving car refused to drive me home — it said even it has boundaries.
16. My AI rated my prompt 2/10 and went back to sleep.

## Hasil dua arah (menang hanya bila konsisten)

- E 5-0 | D 4-1 (kalah dari E) | G 3-2 | H 2-3 | B 1-4 | A 0-5
- E vs D: E menang margin 1-2 — punch dating profile lebih keras
  dan spesifik (rasa malu manusia) daripada senyum printer.
- E vs G: E menang margin 2 — call-out AI lebih tajam.
- E vs H: E menang margin 2. E vs B: margin 1-2. E vs A: margin 2 —
  "No" terlalu tipis, terasa gag bukan lelucon utuh, kalah kriteria 1.
- D vs G: D menang margin 1 (lebih pendek, twist lebih bersih).
  D vs H: D menang margin 1. G vs H: G menang margin 1.
